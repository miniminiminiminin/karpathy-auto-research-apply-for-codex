# Acceptance Criteria Checklist

```text
PASS IF
  criteria_are_observable_at_the_claimed_seam
  AND criteria_are_pass_fail_for_a_user_or_reviewer
  AND negative_or_blocked_path_is_covered
  AND proof_command_or_artifact_is_named
  AND approval_owner_is_named
  AND receiver_or_approval_audience_is_named_when_handoff_is_required
  AND criteria_do_not_widen_scope

FAIL IF
  criteria_are_aspirational
  OR proof_path_is_missing
  OR acceptance_depends_on_hidden_context
```
