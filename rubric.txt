Repository Quality Rubric for This Concept Scaffold

1. Concept clarity
- Does the repository clearly explain the relationship between purpose, rubric, and execution loop?
- Is the distinction between repo-level docs and scaffold-level execution obvious?

2. Execution contract clarity
- Can a user understand when `rubric.txt` may be generated and when it becomes immutable?
- Does the scaffold define concrete logs, outputs, and keep/discard rules?

3. Reusability
- Can a downstream user adapt the scaffold with minimal editing?
- Are templates and placeholders explicit instead of hidden in prose?

4. Honesty of claims
- Does the repo avoid overstating automation that is not actually implemented?
- Are current limitations stated plainly?

5. Extensibility
- Can domain-specific rubric variants be added without breaking the core contract?
- Does the scaffold preserve a stable core while allowing purpose-specific extensions?

6. Documentation quality
- Are the starting steps easy to follow?
- Are the main rules visible in the right files?

Scoring guidance:
- Strong: the repo is understandable and reusable without extra explanation.
- Medium: the concept is promising but some workflow steps still rely on interpretation.
- Weak: the purpose/rubric/run model remains ambiguous or internally inconsistent.
