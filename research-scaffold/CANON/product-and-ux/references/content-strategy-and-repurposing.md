# Content Strategy And Repurposing

Content planning should preserve one core idea across formats.

```text
WORKING_ORDER :=
  1 -> define_audience_and_desired_action
  2 -> choose_2_to_4_content_pillars
  3 -> write_canonical_long_form_message_or_brief
  4 -> set(voice_and_tone_expectations, accessibility_text_implications, translation_readiness)_before_adaptation
  5 -> adapt_into_channel_specific_cuts
  6 -> measure_response_and_feed_learning_into_the_next_brief

REPURPOSING_RULES := PASS IF
  shortening_preserves_the_promise
  AND proof_points_stay_consistent
  AND format_and_tone_change_per_channel_without_changing_the_core_claim
  AND accessibility_text_and_support_wording_stay_aligned_with_the_underlying_ux
  AND copy_stays_translation_ready_when_cross_locale_reuse_is_likely
  AND response_templates_exist_for_likely_confusion_or_objections
```
